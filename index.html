About

Who I am

I am a dedicated researcher with a Master's in Computer Science (Data Science), specializing in Natural Language Processing and Deep Learning. My work revolves around building equitable AI systems for low-resource languages, particularly Bengali and other South Asian languages.

I have published in top-tier venues including ACL and EMNLP, and contributed to open-source NLP toolkits for South Asian languages. As a Teaching Assistant at SUST, I've mentored over 200 students in Machine Learning and Data Structures.

I am actively seeking a fully-funded PhD position where I can push the boundaries of multilingual AI and contribute to making NLP accessible for underrepresented languages.
